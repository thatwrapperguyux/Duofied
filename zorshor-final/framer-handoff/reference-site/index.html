How long does an ad film take?

Most ad films take 3–6 weeks from brief to final cut: a week of pre-production, one to three shoot days and about two weeks of post. Rush timelines are possible — we'll tell you honestly what gets cut.

What does a project cost?

It depends on scale — a reels pack starts well below a TVC. Share your budget range in the plan form and we'll build the plan around it, not the other way round.

Do you shoot outside Mumbai?

Yes. We shoot across India and travel with a lean crew for international shoots. AI ads and motion graphics need no travel at all.

Can you handle scripts and ideas too?

That's usually where we start. Our writers and directors can take you from a one-line idea to a shooting script, or work from a script your agency already has.

What are AI ads, exactly?

Short ads built with AI-assisted visuals and voice, directed and edited by our team. They're fast to make and great for testing hooks before a full shoot.
