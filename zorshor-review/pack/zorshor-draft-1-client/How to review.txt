zorshor website — review files

There are two design directions to compare:
  • zorshor - Premium.html
  • zorshor - Draft 1.html

1. Double-click a file to open it in your browser (Chrome, Safari or Edge).
2. Browse it like a normal website: Home, Socials, Films and Plans are in the top menu.

Leaving comments
  1. Click "Comment" in the black bar at the bottom-right.
  2. Click any part of the page — a heading, an image, a button.
  3. Type what you'd like changed, add your name, then press "Post comment".
     A numbered pin marks the spot. Press Esc to stop commenting.
  4. Click the list icon to see all your comments. You can resolve or delete them there.

Sending your comments back
  • "Copy share link" copies a link that contains all your comments — paste it into
    WhatsApp or email. (It opens with your pins in place once the file is hosted online.)
  • "Copy as text" copies a numbered list you can paste anywhere.
  • "Export file" saves a small .json file — attach it to an email.
Any one of these is enough.

Your comments are saved in this browser, so you can close the file and come back later.
Comments in Premium and Draft 1 are kept separately.
