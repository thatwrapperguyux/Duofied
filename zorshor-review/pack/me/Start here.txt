Reading the client's comments
  A) Live links (recommended): share the Premium / Draft 1 links with the client from the page's
     Share menu (give them comment access). Their comments appear in the comments panel on the same
     link, for you and the client.
  B) Offline files: the client sends you a share link, copied text, or a .json file.
     • Text: just read it — each comment is numbered with the page and section.
     • Link or .json: open the client file (zorshor - Premium.html or Draft 1) in your browser,
       click the list icon at the bottom-right → "Import comments", paste the link or choose
       the file. Pins appear on the page exactly where the client left them.

Notes
  • Page changes play one of three paper-cut "action" transitions in turn (sweep left, rise, sweep right).
  • Chicken Dinner (the brand's secondary font) is used only for the "action" page-change word. The
    included file is a subset taken from the brand kit — install the licensed font in Framer.
  • Placeholders are intentionally empty. There's no copyright line — add the client's when ready.
